LEVEL LANGUAGE

Line or zone?

Beginners often draw a perfect line and expect price to respect it exactly. AshFX teaches areas of interest: price can enter a zone, react, break it or retest it. The goal is context, not certainty.

SUPPORTArea where downside pressure previously found demand.
RESISTANCEArea where upside pressure previously met supply.
DEMANDArea associated with strong buying response.
